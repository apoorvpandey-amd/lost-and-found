Safety & Verification Protocol

Recommended safety procedures for property retrieval exchanges.

Public Meeting Locations

Always coordinate physical item exchanges in supervised, monitored locations such as facility security desks or administrative offices.

Proof of Ownership

Request verifying documentation such as serial numbers, purchase invoices, or unique non-public identifying details.

Financial Caution

Never send monetary advances or courier dispatch charges prior to physical handover and ownership verification.

Data Protection

Do not disclose private financial data, access codes, or government identification numbers during inquiry correspondence.
