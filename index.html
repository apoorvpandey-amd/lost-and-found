Safety & Verification Tips

Follow these trusted practices to keep yourself and your belongings safe.

🏛️

Meet in Public Places

Always arrange exchanges in well-lit public spots like campus security desks, libraries, or station lobbies.

🔒

Verify Proof of Ownership

Ask for unique identifying details such as device serial numbers, distinctive marks, or purchase receipts.

💳

Never Send Advance Money

Legitimate finders will never request courier delivery fees or gift cards in advance before meeting.

🛡️

Protect Confidential Info

Never share sensitive passwords, PINs, or ID numbers during the inquiry or verification process.
